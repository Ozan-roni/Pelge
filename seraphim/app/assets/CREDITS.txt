mockup. Create an original sacred seraph with six intricate layered wings
curling and wrapping around a single calm luminous eye, in the visual
tradition of antique etched heavenly illustrations. Subject placed in the
right half of a wide cinematic 3:2 composition, left 42 percent almost empty
dark charcoal smoke for later UI typography. Black graphite feather detail,
ivory engraving highlights, subtle antique pale gold rim light, restrained
gold halo behind the figure, soft wisps of shadow clouds. Very sophisticated
luxury editorial aesthetic, rich feather texture, no garish colors. All four
edges dissolve naturally into near-black #0C0D10, especially the entire left
edge. The image must feel atmospheric, graceful, majestic, not threatening.
No text, no letters, no watermark, no UI, no frame. Entire original
illustration. Output landscape high resolution.

Prompt final utilisé :
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative wide hero art for a
futuristic luxury software desktop app Seraphim Premium, not an interface
mockup. Brand reference concept: an eye surrounded by six wings of a seraph.
Reimagine as a sophisticated floating chrome and frosted-glass sculptural
seraph emblem, wings are six elegant layered precision-engineered metallic
fins, with subtle translucent violet edges and an eye-like polished obsidian
center. Clear refined geometry, microscopically precise thin machining lines
and delicate orbital halo rings. The wings curve gracefully around the
center, recognizable celestial six-wing structure, not mechanical bird or
robot. Premium titanium, brushed silver, satin chrome, smoke glass. Sparse
atmospheric deep ink-black and violet haze background #0C0D15, soft studio
reflections, pale lilac light, tasteful dark luxury tech product photography.
Wide landscape 3:2 composition, subject entirely on right 50 percent, left
45 percent clean dark negative space for later real UI typography. All edges
fade subtly into background. Sharp exquisite material details with restrained
luminous highlights. No text, no letters, no logos besides the sculptural
seraph, no watermark, no buttons, no user interface.
